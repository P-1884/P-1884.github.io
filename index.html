Philip Holloway

Research Fellow in Strong Gravitational Lensing

I am a research fellow based at the Institute of Cosmology and Gravitation (University of Portsmouth). I am interested in strong gravitational lenses and amongst other things I am experienced in strong lens finding, Bayesian statistics and population-level inference of populations of strong lenses.

I am a member of the Euclid and LSST Collaborations alongside Space Warps, a citizen science collaboration which aims to identify strong gravitational lenses with the help of lay-volunteers.
